Intro:

This is a list of Skyward Sword enemies that I am creating. It will have all in-game descriptions of them in addition to information I will infer, as well as their location and times of occurance.

Usually, when you kill a monster in Skyward sword, a purple cloud shaped like a demon's head appears. For some reason, this does not occur with all monsters, and each monster's section will list if it has the demon's symbol or not.

Remlits

Remlits are peaceful cat-like animals kept as pets by Humans in Skyloft. During the night, however, they turn into monsters and try to attack Link. They are nocturnal, usually seen sleeping during the day, and are able to fly somewhat by flapping their ears like wings.

Health: 2 Sword Swings
Habitats: Skyloft
Demon Symbol: Unkown

Chuchus

Chuchus come in a number of different colors in the game, depending on where they are found. They come in different sizes and may join together to increase size or be split apart into smaller sizes. They sometimes drop Jelly Blobs when they die.

Health: 1 Sword Swing (at smallest size)
Habitats: Skyloft (Green)
Demon Symbol: No

Keese

Keese are large bats that attack Link. They come in a number of variants across the game. Normal Keese living in Skyloft are nocturnal, but their nocturnalness varies in most other regions.

Health: 1-2 Sword Swings
Habitats: Skyloft (Normal)
Demon Symbol: Yes
